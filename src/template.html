How to use

Open a song

With nothing loaded, tap the empty piano roll or drop a .mid file on it. After that, Open .mid (top right) loads another song, and dropping a file on the roll still works.

Piano roll

Each MIDI channel is a lane with its own colour. Drag the roll left or right to move through the song, or tap the timeline under it. If you drag while it plays, it pauses and carries on from where you let go.

Lanes (left side of the roll)

S solos a lane and M mutes it. Lanes you can't hear are dimmed. The note button shows the note the lane is singing now, or its next note; tap it to hear that note. ♪ All plays every audible lane's note from low to high, then holds the chord. Reset clears solo and mute.

Play and Stop

Play/Pause continues from where you are. Stop goes back to the start.

Tempo

− and + change the tempo by 1 BPM; hold them to keep changing it. Tap the number to go back to the file's original tempo, which is shown at the top. The range is half to 1.5 times the original. If the file changes tempo partway through, the whole song is scaled.

Metronome and Count-in

Metronome clicks every beat. Count-in plays one bar of clicks before the music starts when you press Play.

Sound

Synth, Piano, Organ or Choir "Ooh", used for all lanes.

No sound?

On an iPhone, flip the silent switch off and check the volume.

Everything runs in your browser. Files are never uploaded.
